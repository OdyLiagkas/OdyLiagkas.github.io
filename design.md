# Design Philosophy

## Color Palette
- **Primary**: Deep Navy (#1a2332) - Professional, trustworthy, academic
- **Secondary**: Warm Copper (#c17817) - Innovation, warmth, achievement
- **Accent**: Sage Green (#7a8471) - Growth, sustainability (reflecting FunguWear)
- **Neutral**: Warm White (#faf9f7) - Clean, readable, sophisticated
- **Text**: Charcoal (#2d3748) - High contrast, accessible

## Typography
- **Display Font**: "Playfair Display" - Elegant serif for headings, academic prestige
- **Body Font**: "Inter" - Modern, highly readable sans-serif for content
- **Code/Data Font**: "JetBrains Mono" - For technical content and publications

## Visual Language
- **Aesthetic**: Modern academic with sophisticated engineering precision
- **Inspiration**: Cornell University's academic excellence meets cutting-edge biomedical innovation
- **Mood**: Professional, innovative, trustworthy, forward-thinking
- **Imagery Style**: High-quality biomedical research imagery, clean laboratory environments, sophisticated data visualizations

# Visual Effects

## Used Libraries
- **Anime.js**: Smooth entrance animations and micro-interactions
- **ECharts.js**: Professional data visualizations for research metrics
- **p5.js**: Creative coding background effects and interactive elements
- **Splide.js**: Smooth image carousels for project showcases
- **Pixi.js**: Advanced visual effects for hero section

## Effects & Animation
- **Hero Background**: Subtle particle system representing molecular structures
- **Text Effects**: Color cycling emphasis on key achievements, split-by-letter stagger for headings
- **Image Effects**: Ken Burns pan/zoom on hero images, displacement hover effects
- **Scroll Motion**: Gentle reveal animations (16-24px translation, 150-300ms duration)
- **Interactive Elements**: 3D tilt cards for publications, smooth hover transitions

## Styling Approach
- **Layout**: Grid-aligned, scroll-native design with generous white space
- **Sections**: Clear visual hierarchy with consistent spacing
- **Cards**: Subtle shadows and hover effects for depth
- **Navigation**: Fixed header with smooth scroll-to-section behavior
- **Responsive**: Mobile-first design with fluid typography scaling
