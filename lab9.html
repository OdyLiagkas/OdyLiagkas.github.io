To turn these into the World's map I had to turn the measurements into positions in the World Frame. First I turned these measurements into positions in ToF's reference frame and then the car's IMU (as that is where the angle is changing for us to take the measurements)

First I needed to measure the distance between the front ToF, which was used to take the measurements, and the center of the IMU. I measured that as 5cm=50mm=0.164042ft

Thus, the matrix to turn a ToF position into a Car (IMU) position is:

T_car_tof = np.array([
    [1, 0, 0],
    [0, 1, 0.164042],
    [0, 0, 1]
])

Then, to turn that from Car to World frame as the car rotates around its axis the matrix is:

T_world_car = np.array([
    [np.cos(angle), -np.sin(angle), x],
    [np.sin(angle),  np.cos(angle), y],
    [0, 0, 1]
    ])
A couple of notes:

T_car_tof is a 2D identity with the distance from the ToF frame to the center of the IMU.

The car was rotating countercockwise, therefore the rotation was positive. Thus the signs of the rotation matrix component of the T_world_car matrix.

Because the car was only rotating and not moving, since I had aligned the car with the Y axis for θ=0, saying that the car's longitudinal axis was the car's Y axis was fine. After all the IMU's axes are for the robot to know in which direction it's moving. The IMU's and the World's Z axes are aligned either way so the rotation's angle is correct.

In future labs, I will start the car in such a way where the X and Y axes of the world are aligned with the car's for θ=0. There, the front ToF's meaurement would be in the Car's X axis and not Y.

Finally, I had: p_world = T_world_car @ T_car_tof @ p_tof where 

 p_tof = np.array([[0], [dist * 0.00328084], [1]])
where we turn the distance measured into feet.
