## Page Structure

### index.html - Professional Landing
**Purpose**: Immediate impact showcasing expertise and achievements
**Sections**:
- Navigation bar with smooth scroll
- Hero section with generated biomedical research image
- Key highlights: Fulbright Scholar, Cornell M.Eng, Research Publications
- Interactive timeline of major achievements
- Featured projects carousel
- Call-to-action to explore detailed sections

### about.html - Comprehensive Background
**Purpose**: Detailed academic and professional journey
**Sections**:
- Personal introduction and mission statement
- Education timeline (Cornell, NYU, NKUA, Athens College)
- Work experience with detailed descriptions
- Skills and certifications
- Leadership and extracurricular activities
- Awards and recognition

### research.html - Research Portfolio
**Purpose**: Showcase research expertise and publications
**Sections**:
- Current research focus (Tissue Tensioner project)
- AI and cryogenic electron microscopy work
- Publications and presentations
- Research methodology and tools
- Future research interests
- Collaboration and partnerships

### contact.html - Professional Contact
**Purpose**: Easy access to contact information and professional networks
**Sections**:
- Contact information display
- LinkedIn and professional profiles
- Location information
- Professional interests for networking
- Availability for collaborations

## Interactive Elements
- Smooth scroll navigation between sections
- Animated achievement counters
- Interactive research timeline
- Hover effects on publication cards
- Responsive image galleries
- Contact form with validation

## Content Strategy
- Professional tone with academic precision
- Highlight unique achievements (Fulbright, multiple scholarships)
- Emphasize interdisciplinary expertise (biology + engineering + AI)
- Showcase leadership and innovation (FunguWear, publications)
- Demonstrate global perspective (Greece, USA, international collaborations)
